## Summary

Balu's read is right: the chrome is built for a 10 to 12 px world.
Pane-header chips, the 5-hour quota gauge and the sparkline label sit at 9.5 to 10.5 px.
Most icon-only buttons have no visible label and rely on a hover `title`.
The quota gauge has no popover at all, only a native tooltip, so "what does 5h / wk mean and when does it reset" is hidden.
The fix is mostly CSS: raise the chrome type floor to 12 px, the click-target floor to 28 px, and add labels or a visible affordance where a glyph alone is ambiguous.

## Ranked critique per surface

### Pane header (worst offender)

1. Chips are 9.5 to 10 px. Principle: legibility floor, Fitts. Evidence: `probe0.png`, `paneheader-1600-dark.png`. Fix: set chip text to 12 px, height 24 px.
2. Too many peer chips (branch, ctx, diff, state, model, plan, mem, subagents, process, idle) all styled alike. Principle: Hick's law, hierarchy. Fix: show state, branch and diff by default; fold the rest into one "details" popover.
3. The directory suffix ("· acme-api") is muted to near invisible and means "working folder", which nothing says. Fix: slightly larger, tooltip text "Working folder", and match the folder icon used in the menu.
4. Terminal/chat toggle is two tiny glyphs. Fix: segmented control with text labels "Terminal | Chat", 28 px high.
5. Icon buttons carry no labels and have a hover-only colour change. Fix: 28 px targets, keep icons, add visible hover background and a consistent tooltip with shortcut.
6. Close sits beside maximise and "more". Principle: destructive action proximity. Fix: space close away from the others or put it in the menu with a confirm for live agents.

### 5-hour quota gauge

7. 10.5 px text, 4 px track, no click action, no popover. Evidence: `quotagauge.css`, `QuotaGauge.tsx:34`. Fix: 12 px text, 6 px track, click opens a popover with both windows, reset times, source (reported or estimated) and the explanation that is currently in the tooltip string.
8. "5h" and "wk" are jargon. Fix: "5-hour" and "Week" labels, and a percent-used label.
9. Estimated and reported sources look identical. Fix: a small "est." tag when the figure is estimated.

### Top bar

10. Five unlabeled icons (home, bell, explorer, broadcast, theme) plus an unlabeled menu glyph. Evidence: `probe0.png`. Principle: recognition over recall. Fix: add text labels at 1600 px and wider, collapse to icons below.
11. Next to the workspace name sits an unlabeled glyph (looks like a layout or diff icon). Fix: label it or remove it from the bar.
12. Theme toggle cycles light, dark, system with no indication of which is current. Fix: show the current mode in the icon and the tooltip.

### Left rail

13. "WORKSPACES 2" with an unlabeled up-arrow and a plus. Fix: label "New workspace", and say what the arrow does.
14. Workspace rows show tiny count badges (4 with a grid glyph, a dot and a count) with no legend. Fix: one text line, "4 panes, 1 waiting".
15. Rail text is 11 to 13 px; avatar tiles are large relative to the text. Fix: rebalance, raise secondary text to 12 px.

### Launcher (new workspace)

16. Disabled "Create Workspace" with no reason shown. Evidence: `launcher-1600-dark.png`. Fix: inline hint "Choose a folder to continue".
17. Many controls in one card (layout, folder, worktree, setup command, four per-pane rows, move/delete icons). Principle: Hick, progressive disclosure. Fix: keep layout and folder visible; put worktree, setup and per-pane edits under "Customise".
18. Footer helper text is about 11 px and low contrast. Fix: 12 px, secondary text token.
19. Worktree checkbox has a "?" help icon about 14 px. Fix: one plain-English line under it instead of a hover icon.

### Pane menu and Review drawer

20. Pane menu mixes actions and settings (zoom, focus mode, fold commands). Evidence: `panemenu-1600-dark.png`. Fix: group with headers: View, Session, Folder.
21. Review drawer empty state is plain text ("No changes yet") with a long footnote in small grey. Evidence: `review-1600-dark.png`. Fix: centred empty state with an icon and one next step.
22. Review header chip "main +2" is unexplained. Fix: "2 commits ahead of main".

### Notifications and Explorer

23. Bell badge and panel were captured (`notifications-1600-dark.png`) but not critiqued in detail. Marked for the resume pass.
24. Explorer captured (`explorer-1600-dark.png`), not yet critiqued.
