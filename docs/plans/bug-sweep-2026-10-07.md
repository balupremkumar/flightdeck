## Confirmed bugs

### B1 (minor): double-clicking a pane name to rename also maximises the pane
Repro: double-click the pane name (title tooltip says "double-click to rename").
Expected: rename field only.
Actual: the rename field opens and the pane toggles maximised. With 3 panes the other two disappear (`w1b-sw-02-added-renamed.png`, only "Alpha pane" is visible and its button reads Restore).
Cause: PaneView.tsx:912-917 header onDoubleClick ignores `button, input, .pdiff, .ptok, .branch` but not `.pname` (PaneView.tsx:963 sets editing and does not stop the event).
Seen via a synthetic dblclick event, which a real double-click also produces. Needs one real-mouse confirm.

### B2 (minor): pane menu is taller than short windows and is clipped, with no scroll
Repro: open the pane "..." menu at a window about 700 px high (900x700).
Expected: all items reachable.
Actual: items from "Session snapshots" down (Export, Import, font size, Ligatures, clipboard, quiet threshold, Save as default, Close pane) are cut off (`w1b-sw-25-narrow-panemenu.png`).
At 1100 px high the menu is about 992 px, so a 1080p screen with the taskbar (about 950 px usable) clips Close pane too.
Cause: `.pmenu` is `position: fixed` with `overflow: hidden` and no `max-height` (panes.css:222-235).
Fix direction: `max-height: calc(100vh - ...)` and `overflow-y: auto`.

### B3 (minor): Review drawer omits untracked files
Repro: in the scratch repo create `newfile.txt` (untracked) and edit `README.md`, open pane menu > Review changes.
Expected: both listed (the pane header chip logic and the rest of the UI treat new files as changes).
Actual: "1 FILE +2 -0", README.md only (`w1b-sw-19-review.png`).
May be by design (diff against HEAD). If so, say so in the drawer. Not traced.

### B4 (minor, risk to Balu): Canary took foreground for about 1 s during the sweep
Seen once, in `bs-sweep.mjs` (first run), after the narrow, wide and mid resizes while the window was moved with `window.ps1 -Mode move` (NOACTIVATE) and the menus were opened.
Evidence: `w1b-sweep-foreground.jsonl` lines 248-251 (pid 10628, title "Flightdeck Canary, 2 waiting", 4 samples at 250 ms). The harness aborted and tore down.
Matches the known focus grabs in STATE (W1a). Not reproduced in the second sweep, the K11 run or the probe (0 Canary foreground samples in all three).
Likely trigger: a window-geometry change or the "waiting" title and attention update. Not isolated. Treat as unresolved.

## UI and usability observations

Search works ("quiet" gives a hit, "zzzqqq" gives `No settings match "zzzqqq"`). The command palette does offer "Settings: <section>" jumps. A side nav would be easier.
